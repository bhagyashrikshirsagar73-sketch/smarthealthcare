Project Aim & Objectives

Aim: To demonstrate how Constraint Satisfaction Problems and Forward Chaining process illustrative symptom inputs and explain rule-based reasoning in an accessible, visual, and transparent manner.

  • Implement discrete variables V and binary domains {Yes, No} to prune inconsistent diagnostic hypotheses.
  • Implement a data-driven Forward Chaining engine with live rule premise matching and step execution.
  • Provide a real-time output console that instantly displays predicted diseases on symptom toggling.
  • Maintain strict transparency without black-box machine learning opacity.
